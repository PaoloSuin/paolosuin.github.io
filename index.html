Paolo Suin

Astrophysicist · Star formation and numerical simulations

I study how stars form using high-resolution magnetohydrodynamic simulations of molecular clouds. I'm especially interested in stellar feedback: how jets and the radiation of massive stars shape filaments, dense hubs and young clusters, and how efficiently clouds turn into stars.

I studied at the University of Pisa and have worked with colleagues at the Laboratoire d'Astrophysique de Marseille (LAM) and CEA Paris-Saclay.

Research

Stellar feedback

How protostellar jets and ionising radiation from HII regions regulate collapse and set the star formation efficiency per free-fall time.

Filaments and hub-filament systems

The role of the magnetic field and feedback in how filamentary networks form, feed dense hubs, and are eroded by young massive stars.

Star formation laws

Testing the Kennicutt–Schmidt relation by comparing simulated clouds with observations under the same biases.

Young star clusters

How the turbulent gas of the parent molecular cloud perturbs the dynamical evolution of young massive clusters.
